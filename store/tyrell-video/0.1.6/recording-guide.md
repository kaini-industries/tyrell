# Real-device demonstration recording

The first-app review needs a short authentic recording of Tyrell Video running
on a physical CardputerZero. A phone camera recording the handheld and keys is
suitable. This guide prepares that recording; it is not evidence that a video
already exists. The synthetic MP4 below is content to play on the device, not
the required recording itself.

Use the included `demo/TYRELL-DEMO.mp4` and matching `demo/TYRELL-DEMO.srt`.
They are generated test patterns, tones and original text. Copy both into a
folder under `~/Videos` on the Zero. Do not use a personal movie, copyrighted
movie footage, private filenames, passwords or network settings in the recording.
The demo is separate from the unchanged app package.

Record about 45–60 seconds, keeping the physical screen readable:

1. Wake and unlock APPLaunch. Open **Tyrell Video**, navigate to
   `TYRELL-DEMO.mp4`, and press **Enter**. Show the moving pattern and sound on
   the native screen for several seconds. If the demo has ended, Space replays it.
2. While the elapsed time is comfortably before the end, press **Space**.
   Keep the frozen picture and time visible briefly. Press Space to resume;
   playback should continue from that point.
3. Press **T**. Use Down/S until the highlighted row starts with **Subs:**
   (often `Subtitles 1 (SRT)`); press **Enter** to enable it. The `Subtitles:
   Off` row disables captions. Show a caption and the continuing elapsed time.
4. Press **Right/D** to seek forward ten seconds, then **Left/A** to go back.
   Briefly show Up/W or Down/S changing volume.
5. Press **Esc** to return to the file browser, then **Q** to return to
   APPLaunch. Keep that clean return in the recording.

Do not record a claimed 0.1.6 TV test without an attached display. When HDMI is
available for a separate test, show the TV and handheld together; verify TV
sound, correct shapes/aspect fitting, no cursor and responsive controls, then
unplug HDMI for at least 15 seconds. The LCD should receive a paused picture at
the same position. Reconnect, select O and resume; verify TV video and sound.
That physical 0.1.6 test is currently pending.

Review the phone recording before posting. Retain the original recording and
attach it to the eventual Store PR after submission. Do not label simulated
screenshots or a desktop capture as physical-device footage.
