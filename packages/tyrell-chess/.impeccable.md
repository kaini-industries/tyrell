## Design Context

### Users

Cardputer Zero players enjoying quick handheld chess games, pass-and-play,
computer opponents, LAN matches, and tactical puzzles. The keyboard and native
320 × 170 LCD require crisp pieces, concise labels, and clear focus states.

### Brand Personality

Playful, colorful, crafted. The user explicitly selected **playful retro style:
colorful pixel art** on November 15, 2026.

### Aesthetic Direction

A pocket game cartridge: saturated blue and mint board squares, indigo ink,
cream pixel pieces, coral and gold highlights, and small pixel stars. Native
pixel geometry and hard-edged artwork suit the small display. The interface
should feel cheerful and immediately playable, with readable clocks and moves.

### Design Principles

1. Make the board and piece silhouettes immediately readable at native size.
2. Use color for personality and state; reinforce state with outlines and shapes.
3. Keep keyboard focus obvious and show useful controls in context.
4. Use short purposeful piece motion, with a reduced-motion option.
5. Give gameplay priority over decoration and keep text within the LCD bounds.
